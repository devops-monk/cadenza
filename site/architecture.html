1 · The journey

From a sentence
to a song.

Two models share the work. A language model writes the words. YuE2, a 3-billion-parameter music model, composes and sings them.

  1. You Cadenza window

    A description, in any language. Lyrics and a genre are optional.

    → description
  3. Songwriter Gemma 4 · llama.cpp

    Writes a title, style tags and lyrics marked [Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge].

    → lyrics + style
  5. Composer YuE2 · writing half

    Writes a score with melody and chords, then the song as codes: 25 per second.

    → score + codes
  7. Performer YuE2 · painting half

    Turns the codes into sound by flow matching: noise refined, step by step, into every frame at once.

    → latents
  9. Sound YuE2 VAE decoder

    Decodes 48 kHz stereo audio, saved as an MP3 in your library.

    → MP3

The score travels with every song. Open a track in the app to read its lyrics, style and score.

2 · The system

Three programs.
One computer.

Cadenza is a desktop app that starts two local servers and talks to them over your own machine's loopback address. Nothing listens to the outside world.

3 · The stages

What you see,
and what happens.

Each song is a job. These are the stages its card shows while you wait, and the work behind each one.

  1. 1

    Writing lyrics

    The Rust core starts llama-server with your Gemma model, or uses Ollama or another server, and asks for a title, style tags and lyrics as JSON. Skipped when you bring your own lyrics and style.

  2. 2

    Starting the music engine

    On computers under 32 GB of memory the language model is stopped first by default, so the two never fill memory together. Then yue-server starts with YuE2 and its VAE.

  3. +

    Listening to the recording remakes only

    Your recording is turned into a score or into YuE2's codes. See Remakes.

  4. 3

    Composing & singing

    One request to the engine: write the score, write the codes, paint the sound, decode the audio. The core polls the engine and shows its progress.

  5. 4

    Saving

    The engine returns the audio with the recipe that made it. Both go to your library, ready to play, rename, save or remake.

One song is made at a time; the next waits its turn. Cancel stops a job at its next step.

4 · The GPU

One model, two halves,
taking turns.

YuE2 is one transformer with two halves that share a memory of everything written so far: the KV cache. To fit a 16 GB laptop, only one part sits on the GPU at a time.

Writing halfscore, then codes, one token at a time

KV cachestays put, so the painting half reads what the writing half left and nothing is computed twice

With 32 GB of memory or more, Cadenza keeps everything loaded instead, for faster songs back to back.

5 · Remakes and variations

Start from a song
you already have.

Two extra models listen to a recording so YuE2 can sing it again. Both hear it through MERT-v2, an encoder trained on music.

Same melody, new style

  1. Your recording
  2. MERT-v2 + SheetSage2
  3. A score of its melody
  4. YuE2 sings it in your style

Continue from a section

  1. A part of your recording
  2. MERT-v2 + realaudio tokenizer
  3. YuE2's own codes
  4. The new song opens with it, then carries on

New variation

  1. A song you made
  2. Its stored codes
  3. A new sound seed
  4. Only the painting half and VAE run again

A variation keeps the composition and the singing and changes the sound, in a fraction of the time.
